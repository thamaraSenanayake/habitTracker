Account & Associated Data Deletion

At HabitFlow, we respect your privacy and provide complete control over your personal data. You can delete your cloud synchronization account and all associated habit tracking details at any time.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

When you request account deletion, all synced information is permanently and irreversibly purged from our servers, including:

  • Your account authentication profile (email address and login credentials).
  • Your user profile information (display name, streaks, and settings).
  • All synced habits, completed calendar logs, counters, and statistics.

How to Delete Your Account

Method 1: In-App (Instant)

The fastest way to delete your data is directly inside the HabitFlow mobile application:

  • Open the HabitFlow app.
  • Go to the Settings screen (gear icon).
  • Scroll to the bottom and click on the Delete Account / Reset Profile button.
  • Confirm the request. All cloud and local database records will be erased instantly.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request (Up to 7 Days)
