Privacy Policy • Last Updated: August 2026

At HabitFlow, your privacy is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy details how we handle, secure, and respect your sensitive user and device data.

1. Local-First Data Storage

HabitFlow prioritizes your privacy by keeping your data on your device. All habits, completion history, streaks, and settings are saved locally using a secure SQLite database. We do not require passwords to be stored locally to ensure maximum security.

2. Optional Cloud Sync

You have full control over your data. By enabling "Account & Cloud Sync" in the settings, your profile picture, username, and habit logs will sync with Google Firebase Firestore to support multiple devices. Toggling this option off immediately stops cloud uploads, keeping your logs entirely offline.

3. Local Notifications

Reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device via the flutter_local_notifications plugin. We do not use remote push servers or trackers to deliver these reminders, and all scheduling is calculated locally using timezone offsets.

4. Data Export & Rights

You own your data. At any time, you can export your entire habit list and completion history in JSON format via the "Export Data" button in your settings to save it or share it elsewhere. You can also request complete account and data deletion at any time.

5. Information Security

We employ standard device sandboxing and safety measures. No analytical trackers, third-party advertisement scripts, or behavioral tracking SDKs are present in HabitFlow.
